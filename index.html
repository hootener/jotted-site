Handwriting notes on an infinite canvas.

A pen, a black page that never ends, and nothing in the way. Made for a stylus, happy with a mouse.

Download Try it in the browser

Built for a pen

Pressure-sensitive strokes with input smoothing, so handwriting looks like handwriting. Palm touches are ignored while you write.

Infinite canvas

Pan with a finger or the space bar, pinch or scroll to zoom. Strokes stay crisp at any magnification.

Six colors, no menus

White, yellow, pink, green, blue and purple, chosen to sit well on black. Arrow keys flip between colors and pen weights.

Paper when you want it

Toggle a subtle grid, ruled lines, or dots behind your writing. Saved with each jot.

Yours, locally

Every jot is a plain JSON file on your disk (or in your browser). No account, no sync, no server.

Fast to clear, safe to undo

One key clears the page for the next thought. Undo brings it back if you change your mind.
