Handwritten notes. None of the bloat.

A pen and a black page that goes on forever. No shapes, no text boxes, no toolbar three menus deep. Open it, write the thing down, get on with your day.

Download Try it in the browser

Just start writing

There is one tool. It is a pen. It knows how hard you are pressing and it smooths out the wobble. When the thought is done, one key clears the page. Undo brings it back if the thought wasn't.

Great on a screenshare

Press H and every scrap of interface disappears. Just your handwriting on black, which is the only part anyone watching cares about.

Your machine. Your notes.

Everything lives on your own disk as plain files. No cloud, no signup, no “we’ve updated our privacy policy”. No problem.

No color picker. You’re welcome.

Six colors that look good on black, chosen for you. Tap a dot or hit a number. You will never agonize over a hex code again.

No typing. On purpose.

No text tool, no shape tool, no layers, no templates. If you wanted those, you already have an app for that. This is the other one.

Lined paper, if you insist

A faint grid, ruled lines, or dots behind your writing. Or nothing. Nothing is the default.
